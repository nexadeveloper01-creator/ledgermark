Trust tech — fake vs genuine · ledger integrity

Differentiator
Screen /field · /consoleAccount officer@ · admin@

"The police field terminal scans a UID and shows only genuine-or-not — in the field that is the one thing that matters. If it's a Conia Lab UID in the ledger it's genuine; if not, it's counterfeit. And the ledger is a hash chain, which means not even we can go back and change it."

  1. Police /field: scan a genuine code → ✓ GENUINE
  2. Scan an arbitrary off-ledger code → ✕ COUNTERFEIT instantly
  3. Console Ledger status: "No tampering detected" integrity + anchoring
CoreEnforcement is just two outcomes — genuine / counterfeit, no complex verdicts or reports. "Blockchain trust" proven by a tamper-proof ledger.
